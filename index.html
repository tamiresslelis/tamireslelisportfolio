Uai, from Minas Gerais to the world

It's a pleasure to meet you, uai!

When I was studying computer engineering, the most common mindset was build any site or app to make the code compile and work. At that time, little was said about the importance of usability.

I did my thesis about long-term financing and developed an app focusing on teaching people about it. To validate the App, I needed to do in-depth interviews and usability testing with 14 people, and the result of all of this was me jumping head first into UX.

In 2018, I moved to another city and got my first job as a front-end developer, but my curiosity about UX/UI design only grew.  I found an opportunity and applied. Then started my career as UX & UI Designer.

That's the reason I have chosen to create user-centric, delightful systems and apps.  When I’m not designing, you can find me watching tv, reading, traveling, or annoying my dog.
